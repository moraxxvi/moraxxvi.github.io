moy-unik

Moy Unik tool

A student dashboard on top of Canvas

Problem. Canvas is awkward in small ways. Just to check a grade you have to open the course, find the grades section in the menu, scroll to the bottom and work out the total yourself. Deadlines are the same: the urgent assignment is buried among dozens of others.

A page for Turan University students that puts everything on one screen: what is due and when, current grades, whether you have enough points to be admitted to exams, and your GPA. It includes a “how much do I need on the exam” calculator and Telegram reminders. Works on desktop and phone.

Google Apps Script, JavaScript, Canvas API

Sign-in requires a university email; the screenshot shows sample data. The interface is in Russian.

AI assistant for an online store

Problem. For someone who isn't into tech, choosing a product is hard: there are thousands of reviews, and online video reviews contradict each other. The service reads real customers' reviews for them and shows what matters — what the product does well and what people complain about.

A university project at Turan University. The program sorts customer reviews by topic — camera, battery, heating — and detects whether each is praised or criticised. A chat answers questions about the product based on those reviews.
